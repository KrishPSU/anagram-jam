How to play

  • Rooms. Type a nickname, tap Create a room and send your friends the 5-letter code or the invite link. You need at least 2 players. The round starts when everyone has tapped I'm ready. Spell a word that uses every letter of each scramble: the hidden word is worth 2 points, and any other real word from the same letters is worth 1. Each word has 30 seconds. The round ends as soon as someone gets through all 10 words, and the most points wins.
  • Solo. Two minutes to unscramble as many words as you can, and the clock never stops. Answer in under 3 seconds for a bonus point, and every 3 of those earns a hint. Your best score goes on the leaderboard.
  • Daily Jam. Three words a day (5, 7 and 9 letters), the same for everyone, with 3 tries at each and no hints. New words arrive at midnight where you are.
  • Practice. No clock and no score. If you're stuck, tap 🤷 to see the word.

Common questions

  • I can't get into a room. Check the 5-letter code. If it's right but you see "No room found", or an invite link opens the home page with no code filled in, the room has closed. A room closes straight away when the last player taps 🏠 and leaves, or within about a minute after everyone just closes the game. Just make a new room.
  • The app says "Can't reach Anagram Jam". Check that you're online. If you are, the game itself may be having trouble. Either way, the app keeps trying and comes back by itself as soon as it can, or you can tap Try again.
  • My scores or streak aren't in the iPhone app. The app keeps its own data, separate from Safari, so it starts fresh. Your old solo score is still on the leaderboard.
  • How do I turn off sound and vibration? Tap the speaker button in a game. It turns off the music, the sound effects and the vibration.
  • Can I change my nickname? Yes, on the home page. On the leaderboard it changes after your next solo game where you score.

Report a player name

Nicknames that look rude are shown as "Player" on the solo leaderboard automatically. If you see an offensive one anywhere, in a room or on the leaderboard, email krishschavan@gmail.com with the name and where you saw it (the room code, or "leaderboard"). We'll take it off the leaderboard and, while the room is still open, remove that player from it. If that player plays Solo again under the same name, it comes back, so tell us if you see it again.
