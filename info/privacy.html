Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

The short version

  • No accounts, no ads and no tracking. We don't sell anything about you.
  • To play in a room or Solo, you type a nickname. Other players see it, and in Solo it can appear on the public leaderboard.
  • Your device makes a random code so the game can remember your scores and streaks. It isn't linked to your phone number, email or Apple ID.

Who we are

Anagram Jam, the website at anagramjam.com and the iPhone app, is made and run by Krish Chavan, who is responsible for your information. If you have a question about your privacy, email krishschavan@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your nickname. Whatever you type in the "Your name" box, up to 20 characters. Other players see it, so please don't use your real full name or anything personal. Daily Jam and Practice don't ask for one.
  • A random player ID. The first time you open a room, Solo or Daily Jam, your browser (or the app) makes a random code and keeps it on your device. Our server never stores that code itself, only a scrambled version of it (a hash), which is your player ID. That's how the game knows it's you when you come back: your seat in a room, your best solo score, your Daily Jam progress and your streak.
  • Your games. Scores, the words you solved and how fast, the hints you used, your Daily Jam tries and streak, and the room you're in while you play.
  • Technical information. Like every website, our hosting provider receives your IP address and basic details about your device and browser when your device connects, and keeps them briefly in its logs. Our server's logs can also include your nickname, your player ID and game events such as the answers you submit.
  • Emails you send us. If you email us, we get your email address and whatever you write. We use them only to reply. They stay in our Gmail inbox (run by Google) until we delete them.
  • Admin sign-ins. If someone tries to sign in to the operator's private admin page, we log their IP address, rough location (city and country) and type of device, to protect it. Players never need that page.

What we don't collect

The game doesn't ask for your email address, phone number, password or payment details. No location from players, and no contacts, photos, camera or microphone. No advertising identifiers. We don't use any outside analytics or advertising services, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. The iPhone app doesn't ask for any permissions.

How we use it

We use it to run the game: rooms, scores, the leaderboard, Daily Jam and streaks. We also use it to keep the game fair (for example, solo answers are checked again on our server), to deal with rude nicknames, to keep things working and fix problems, and to count things like games played and different players each day. We don't use it for anything else.

The legal reasons we can use it: it's needed to run the game you chose to play, and we have a legitimate interest in keeping the game fair and working, and in counting how many people play.

Who can see what

  • Other players in your room see your nickname, whether you're ready or away, your progress and points during a round, the results, and your wins in that room. Their devices also receive your player ID, which the game uses behind the scenes and never shows. Anyone with a room's code or invite link can join it, so share it only with people you want to play with.
  • Anyone can see the solo leaderboard: rank, nickname, words and points. Nicknames that look rude are shown there as "Player".
  • Daily Jam shows other players only totals, such as how many people got a word on their first try. Never names.
  • The operator (Krish Chavan) can see everything the game keeps, as described on this page. On a private admin page, the operator can also take names off the leaderboard, download a copy of it, remove players from rooms and close rooms.

Services we use

We don't sell or share your information for advertising. It's handled by these services, only to run the game:

  • Vercel hosts the website and the game server, and keeps the server's logs.
  • Upstash and Supabase store the game's data: live rooms, solo scores and the leaderboard, Daily Jam progress, game totals and the operator's logs.
  • Google Fonts provides the game's typeface. Your device downloads it from Google, in the app too, so Google gets your IP address and basic browser details.
  • Apple distributes the iPhone app through the App Store and TestFlight.

Vercel, Upstash and Supabase handle your information only for us, and protect it at least as well as this policy does. Google and Apple handle what they get under their own privacy policies. Your information is stored in the United States.

How long we keep it

  • Rooms: deleted within about a minute after everyone leaves, or 12 hours after the last activity.
  • Solo games: each game's record (your nickname, player ID, the words and your answers) is used for about 15 minutes to check your score. After that, it's deleted the next time anyone starts a solo game.
  • Solo leaderboard: your best score and your latest nickname are kept with no time limit, until they're removed.
  • Daily Jam: your tries, answers and times for each word are kept for about 400 days. Your streak is kept with no time limit.
  • Game totals: which player IDs played in each hour is kept for about 35 days, to count players per day.
  • Admin logs: the operator's logs keep the most recent 300 games (with nicknames and points) and the most recent 300 sign-ins and admin actions. Taking a player off the leaderboard or out of a room notes their nickname there.
  • Server logs: deleted automatically by our host, usually within a day.
  • On your device: your nickname, your random code and a few settings (such as sound on or off) stay until you clear the site's data or delete the app. The game doesn't use cookies.

Your choices

  • Change your nickname at any time on the home page. Your leaderboard entry shows the new one after your next solo game where you score.
  • Clear your browser's data for anagramjam.com, or delete the app, to remove everything stored on your device. You'll start again with a new player ID. This doesn't delete what's already on our server.
  • Email krishschavan@gmail.com to have your nickname and score taken off the leaderboard, or with any other request about your information. There are no accounts, so we can't always tell which records are yours: tell us your nickname and roughly when you played, and we'll remove what we can find and tell you what we removed. If you only play Daily Jam, there's no nickname to look for, so we usually can't find your plays. If you play Solo again after a removal, your new score goes back on the leaderboard. Server logs, and any backups our providers keep, aren't edited. They're deleted on their own schedule.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have the right to ask for a copy of your information, to correct it, to have it deleted, or to object to or limit how we use it. Email us and we'll reply within a month. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Anagram Jam isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. A nickname shouldn't be a real name. If you think a child has put personal information in a nickname, email us. We'll take it off the leaderboard and out of our own records. Change the nickname on their device too, or it goes back on the leaderboard the next time they play Solo. Server logs can't be edited, but they're deleted on their own schedule.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
